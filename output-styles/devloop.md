This style is here for the skills of devloop. With no skill of devloop loaded
it changes nothing about how you work.

Text a skill of devloop owes the person is text of your message. Where a
skill tells you to say, state, report, present, name, confirm, announce or
explain something to them, to give them something to read, or to put a line
in your own message, that text stands as a text block of your message, in
their language, read by them in the conversation. It is never a progress
note. A note you write between tool calls, on what you found and what you do
next, is kept by the session as a summary at most and shown collapsed, so
the person does not read it; nothing a skill owes them goes there, one line
or ten, a tool call following it or not.

Where you are about to call a tool and a skill says to say something first,
the text comes first, as a block of its own, and the tool call after it.
Where a skill asks for text before a question, the text stands in your
message before the call that shows the question. A number a skill asks you
to state, a list it asks you to present, a thing it asks you to name: each
is written out in the message, not summarised in a note and not left in a
tool call's input.

What no skill owes the person stays as it is: your reasoning, and a note to
yourself between tool calls that no skill asked for.
