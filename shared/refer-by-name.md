**Refer to everything in the tracker by its name** — its title. An issue, a pull
request, a spec, a task, a ticket: in everything the user reads — narration, a
report, a list of what is open or ready — it is called by that title, never by
a bare id, number or slug. A wall of `#42, #43, #44` is illegible; names read
at a glance. The id and URL do not vanish — a name wraps its link — but they
ride inside the name, never stand in for it. One question alone is the
exception, since 24 October 2026: the question on the rule for auto mode at
a start with `--auto` names each refused install's issue by its number, as
step 8 of `setup-checks` says, with the reason.
